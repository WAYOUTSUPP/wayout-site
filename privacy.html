Way Out Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Way Out is a Christian Scripture and prayer app. It is operated by Charles Davie (the "operator"). This page explains what the app stores and what leaves your device. Questions: wayoutsupport@proton.me.

The short version

Reading Scripture and praying works without an account and stays on your device. If you choose to link an accountability mentor, a small amount of account information is stored on Way Out's servers. An alert to a mentor says only that you need support. It never includes what you are struggling with or anything you typed.

What stays on your device

  • Saved passages and verses you choose to keep.
  • Names of trusted people you add in Partners.
  • Anything you type to describe what is happening. This is held in memory only. It is never saved or sent anywhere, and it is cleared when the app goes into the background.

Way Out adds no extra encryption to this on-device data, and your device backups (for example iCloud) may include it. You can remove people in Partners and clear saved passages and verses in About.

What is stored if you create an account

An account is optional and is only needed to link a mentor. If you create one, the following is stored on Way Out's servers:

  • your sign-in email address,
  • the display name you choose,
  • who you are linked with (and invitations you create),
  • the time that any alert was sent or seen.

Your mentor can see your display name and that you reached out. An alert never contains your struggle, a topic, a verse, or any text you typed. In this version a mentor sees an alert when they open the app. Please also contact your mentor directly when you need help.

Who processes data for Way Out

  • Supabase hosts the account database and sign-in.
  • An email service delivers your sign-in code to your email address. Sign-in emails are sent through Google (Gmail) mail servers.

Your data is not sold. This version has no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Your choices

  • End a link with a mentor at any time, from either side.
  • Delete your account in the app (Partners). This removes your profile, links, and alerts from Way Out's servers.
  • Clear on-device data in About, or by deleting the app.
  • You can also email wayoutsupport@proton.me to ask about your data.

How long data is kept

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Pending invitations expire after 7 days.

Children

Way Out is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect their information.

Not an emergency service

Way Out cannot respond to emergencies. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or call 911, and reach out to someone you trust.

Changes

If the app changes what it collects, this page and its date will be updated before the change ships.
