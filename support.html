Common questions

I did not get my sign-in code. Check your junk or spam folder. You can request a new code after 60 seconds.

How do I link a mentor? In the Partners tab, create an invitation and send the code to your mentor. They sign in, enter the code, and you are linked. Codes expire after 7 days.

How do I end a link? Either person can end it in the Partners tab.

How do I delete my account? In the Partners tab, choose delete account. This removes your profile, links, and alerts from Way Out's servers.

Does my mentor see what I am struggling with? No. An alert only says you need support.

Does the app work offline? Yes. The complete World English Bible and the prayers are built in.
